01 Token Architecture

Tokens follow a two-layer system. Primitives (--_*) hold raw values and are never used directly in components. Semantic tokens reference primitives and describe what a design decision does, not just what it is. Change a primitive once — every semantic token that references it updates automatically.

Semantic Token Resolves To Design Intent
